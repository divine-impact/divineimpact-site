The Gap
The lead you lose is the one nobody had time for.

Your team returns the calls they catch. The leakage is somewhere else: the quote request that arrives at 4:50 on a Friday, the call that goes to voicemail while a producer is on another line, the web lead that lands Saturday morning.

On a comparison-shopped lead, the winner is usually the agency that gives a real answer first — not the one with the lowest price. That lead has already filled out two other forms.

Illustrative math

$1,850 average annual premium × 12% commission × about 6¾ years of retention ≈ $1,500 in lifetime commission from a single policyholder.

Illustration only. Your own numbers will differ — we measure them before we start.

What We Install and Operate
Two systems. One job: nobody who wanted to talk gets left waiting.

⚡

Speed-to-Lead System

A new lead comes in from your website form, quote request, or lead ad. Within 60 seconds they get a text with your agency's name and a link to book time with you. If they don't answer, a short follow-up sequence continues — right away, 30 minutes, 4 hours, and 24 hours later. The moment they reply, the automated texts stop and your team takes over the conversation.

☎

Missed Call Text-Back System

A call goes unanswered and the caller gets a text within a minute with a link to book. The same system sends appointment confirmations and reminders, offers a one-tap reschedule, and follows up automatically after a no-show.

What it does not do

  • It does not replace your staff. Your producers stop doing the part of the job that was never worth their time, and more of the people who wanted to talk actually reach one.
  • It does not hold conversations, quote, or answer coverage questions. Every message is pre-written, you approve the wording before anything goes live, and anything said on your behalf is something you've already seen.
  • It does not run unattended. A person watches it, and you hear from us if something breaks.
How It Works
From first call to first weekly report.
  1. Discovery call. We ask how leads and missed calls are handled at your agency today. If the numbers don't justify it, we'll tell you.
  2. Your baseline. Before launch, we document (together) your current lead-to-policyholder conversion and missed-call recovery over the prior 60 days. This is the yardstick everything is measured against.
  3. Build and launch. We configure the systems with your agency's name, booking link, and message wording, test every trigger, and handle the carrier registration that business texting requires. Your team gets a brief but important training walkthrough.
  4. Monitoring and reporting. Automated checks watch for failed deliveries and broken triggers. A written performance report arrives every Saturday: leads contacted within 60 seconds, replies, bookings, pipeline, and estimated revenue impact.
The Return
Priced to pay for itself multiple times — and measured so you can see it.

That's our target, measured against your own numbers, not a guarantee.

Our pricing is set against the value the systems deliver to your agency, not against our hours. Three things make that real:

  • A documented starting point. Results are measured against your own baseline. Policyholders you would have won anyway are not credited to us.
  • A weekly scorecard. Every Saturday you see the leads, the response times, the bookings, and an estimate of the revenue impact, in plain numbers.
  • A clear target. We aim for roughly seven times the monthly fee in lifetime policyholder value, measured against your baseline. That is a target, not a guarantee, and it builds as new policyholders stay on your books — expect the full picture over months, not weeks.

Fit
We say who this isn't for.

A good fit

  • You get at least 60 inbound leads or missed calls a month.
  • A real share of your new business is comparison shoppers who are also talking to other agencies.
  • You'd pick up the phone if a client called you Saturday at 9 PM — and you want your lead follow-up held to that standard.

A partial fit

If your business is mostly referrals, the Speed-to-Lead System won't earn its keep. The Missed Call Text-Back System still can: a referral who calls and reaches voicemail is the most expensive voicemail you'll take.

Not a fit

Agencies below about 60 leads or missed calls a month. We'll tell you on the first call.

We work with a small number of agencies at a time, and we choose as carefully as you do.
